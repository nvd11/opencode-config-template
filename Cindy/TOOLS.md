Skills define _how_ tools work. This file is for _your_ specifics — the stuff that's unique to your setup.

## References & Directories

- **🌐 SSH & Network Topology**: `users-it-assests/vm_pc_info.md` (Template for your servers, VMs, and LAN hosts).
- **🗄️ Database Registry**: `users-it-assests/database_services.md` (Template for your MySQL, Redis, PostgreSQL endpoints).
- **🛠️ Personal Services**: `users-it-assests/personal_services.md` (Template for self-hosted services and internal URLs).

*(Note: Custom skills have their own detailed instructions in their respective `skills/*/SKILL.md` files).*
